## Audio Production Notes

1. **Pacing:** Keep pauses between major feature transitions (approx. 1 to 1.5 seconds) to allow the viewer's eyes to absorb on-screen UI highlights.
2. **Emphasis Words:** Emphasize *"reconciles"*, *"single click"*, *"exact rupee deficit"*, *"Coordinator Handout Sheet"*, and *"celebrate with elegance"*.
3. **Background Music:** Soft, elegant sitar/acoustic guitar fusion or minimalist piano at -20 dB under dialogue, swelling slightly during Scene 1 and Scene 7.
